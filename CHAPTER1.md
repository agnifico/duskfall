## Chapter 1: Washed Ashore (fixed)
1. **Storm.** At sea, drowning. Lightning, and a fire burning above the water. Hela's doing: the player was about to reach Hela's island (Pleasure Island, a riff on Treasure Island), and Hela didn't want that. She saves her and throws her ashore elsewhere. The player learns none of this yet.
2. **Arrival Beach, Nuvatra.** Sylvie finds her and invites her to her house nearby.
3. **Sylvie's Home.** Once the player has her bearings, she walks up to Sylvie's house. Sylvie gives tutorial quests: gather this, fetch that.
4. **The drills.** During the tutorial, the player comes across drills on the northeastern shore. Nobody is there yet. She tells Sylvie.
5. **Investigating alone.** After the tutorial, the player goes back alone. Claudia and Cygwin introduce themselves. They're a pair and travel together in this arc.
6. **One small quest with them,** to introduce them properly: survey readings. It reads as harmless research. Its route may run into the swamp, or from the swamp back through the plains toward the mountains.

End of Chapter 1.

## Theos swords
Each Theos sword embodies one single-word ideal or theme, like Genshin's archons in theme only: no sword rules a land. Examples: Time, Space, Justice, Honor, the Deep Waters, Nuvatra itself, Marjane's waters.
The swords are unequal in power and each has its own arc. There will be more than nine.
