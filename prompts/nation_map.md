# Growing the Nuvatra map (Gemini)

Attach `extras/nation_canvas_nuvatra.png` (made by `tools/make_nation_canvas.py`) and paste the prompt. Put the result in `extras/`; `tools/compose_nation.py` pastes the original Nuvatra back on top at full size.

```
This image is the layout for a painted fantasy map. The large island in the middle is finished: keep it exactly as it is, every coastline, mountain, building, river and colour unchanged, and keep the faint turtle shape in the water south of it. Paint everything around it in the same style, colours, light and camera angle: hand-painted and detailed, seen from high above at a slight angle, like a video game's world map. No text, no labels, no icons, no border, no frame.

All the plain dark blue is open sea: deep blue water with a gentle swell, and lighter turquoise shallows only around coasts. The flat coloured shapes are new islands; keep each one where it is, at roughly that size and shape:
- The dark red shape to the north: Hela's island. Black volcanic rock risen straight out of the sea, ringed by dark red sand beaches, palm trees and lit torches. On it, a decadent palace of black stone and gold with terraces and steaming hot springs, and at the island's heart a huge ring-shaped stone gateway standing over a pool of deep red light.
- The grey shape to the west: the Saints' forward base. A rocky island fortified into a harbour: stone sea walls, docks, cranes, warehouses, a lighthouse, and red-and-teal drilling machinery. Banners show a golden sun on white.
- The white shapes to the south: a cluster of icy islands that shouldn't be this far south. Glaciers, blue ice cliffs, snowfields, frozen shelves running into the sea, and one steaming warm spring.
Landscape, 16:9.
```
