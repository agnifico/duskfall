# Enemy prompts (Gemini)

New enemies are drawn to sit beside djv2's enemy sprites until all of them are redrawn in the house style.
Attach one djv2 enemy as the style reference (`game/public/sprites/djv2/toxolotl.png` or `bear1.png`).
Flat magenta background, no shadow: `tools/cut_sheets.py` keys it out, and the game adds the shadow.

## Pinchwarden (common, beach, level 1)
> A game enemy sprite matching the style of the attached image: the Pinchwarden, a small beach crab that has appointed itself guard of the shore. It's trying very hard to look threatening: claws raised, standing tall, maybe a bit of shell or driftwood worn like armour. More brave than dangerous. One creature, full body, on a flat magenta #FF00FF background, no shadow, no ground.

## Grumbletusk (common, beach and plains, level 3)
> A game enemy sprite matching the style of the attached image: the Grumbletusk, a stocky, bristly young boar, always huffing and ready to charge, small tusks, very determined, a bit ridiculous. It means business, it just isn't very good at it. One creature, full body, on a flat magenta #FF00FF background, no shadow, no ground.
