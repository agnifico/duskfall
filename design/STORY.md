# Story skeleton

**Duskfall: Dawn's Journey.** It's the sequel to Dawn's Journey, so the main title is Duskfall.
This is Agni's outline from 2026-11-08: the skeleton, not the script. `CHAPTER1.md` holds the older chapter draft.

## The secret (a spoiler, never stated early)
- The heroine is part of **Hela**, a mirror of her. They are counterparts, yin and yang.
- Hela keeps the **Lucifer** side: the light-bringer, the morning star. She purged the good part of herself so she could go to war with the world, and that is why she is on Earth.
- The heroine's name always means *dawn* or *morning*, so the story keeps hinting that she is the dawn. In the end the dawn, the morning star, turns out to be Hela as well.
- **Still open:** why the two are separate beings, and what links them.

## The powers above the island
- **Solis Saints:** a Templar-like order. They believe in control and manipulation, and in engineering society to make it "better".
- **Guinevere:** the main antagonist on Nuvatra and the mastermind of the drill. She is cool, calm and resolved, with bursts of anger and entitlement. She is powerful and has commanders under her, and, as in Dawn's Journey, she takes to the heroine as a strong, capable warrior. She is a high commander, not the head of the order.
- **Gabriella, the Dark Goddess:** the one Guinevere answers to. She is named after the angel Gabriel, Lucifer's counterpart. She appears all light, holy and heavenly, and wants complete domination of the world.
- **Claudia and Cygwin:** seasoned commanders at the drill. They are cordial and friendly, because they know how to make friends wherever they go. They are only following orders and believe the job is a land survey.

## Arc 1: the beach (levels 1–5, then on to ~10 in the same three areas)
*Revised 2026-11-08.*
1. **Night, storm at sea.** She is drowning, with brief moments of consciousness, then flashes of fire and thunder. That is Hela using her powers to get her to safety, either by carrying her or by sending her off on a gust of wind and water.
2. **Morning, Arrival Beach.** The game opens with her on the beach and **Sylvie already there**. They talk.
3. **When the talk ends, riled common enemies (crabs, toxolotls) converge on them.** Three of them: they come at her, so she has to fight, even though she and Sylvie could otherwise just leave. Beating them takes her to **level 2**.
4. **Sylvie talks again:** "Let's get to my house, it's safe there." The scene **switches straight to Sylvie's Home.**
5. **At the house:** Sylvie asks her name, and the name picker opens. From then on she can rename herself freely, any time.
6. **Sylvie's two quests:**
   - **Thinning the Wilds:** clear monsters around Sylvie's house and on Arrival Beach. Sylvie gives her the **first weapon** as she heads out.
   - **Firewood:** gather wood.
   These take her to about level 4; level 5 comes from fighting by choice.
7. **Levels 5–10 stay in the same three areas:** Arrival Beach, Sylvie's Home and the Pine Grove (it opens in this stretch). Kills come easy at first and slow down toward level 10. Opening a fourth region by level 5 would be too much for a new player.
- **Player choices steer the story** where possible. The first one: Sylvie offers a **sword, a bow or a staff** (2026-11-08: the staff is back, as a catalyst with its own spell; see COMBAT.md). Earlier: a **sword or a bow** (melee or ranged; both physical). *Changed 2026-11-08 from sword or staff:* a staff is a trap early, because basic attacks are physical.
- **Infusion is a cornerstone, and it comes from an optional Heidi quest.** Infusion makes basic attacks elemental, so everything elemental sits behind it. The lesson: pay attention to the smaller characters.
- **Areas can spawn stronger enemies as the player levels**: each area gets a level band (decided 2026-11-08). That lets one area serve twice: the Drill could have a low-level visit and a high-level return.

## Arc 2: the drill (levels 15–20/25)
- After her two quests, Sylvie sends the heroine to see what is going on at the drill.
- The drill stands in the middle of a small village (as the art shows). The villagers are still around and clearly annoyed.
- Claudia and Cygwin give her small jobs, and she comes to like them. None of them knows the dark part.
- **The truth:** the "land survey" *is* the scheme. The drill stays and will be used later. The Saints are causing chaos so the inhabitants leave or crowd into one area, giving the Saints full control of the island. Guinevere planned all of it.
- *(Levels 5–15 come between arcs 1 and 2: the plains and the Pine Grove. Their quests are still to plan.)*

## Arc 3: the mountains (levels 25+)
- Sylvie raises what's wrong on the island:
  - The island used to be peaceful, and now the animals are acting out far more than normal.
  - There are flashes of thunder and lightning off the north coast, from an island that seems to have popped up out of nowhere: Hela's Island.
  - It rains on the northern shores nearly every night, with repeated storms.
  - Someone on the island might know what is going on.
- She sends the heroine to the **mountain cave**, where **Veres** (from Dawn's Journey) lives.
- The mountains are a high-level area, 25 and up, and need proper gear. By then the heroine should have one specialised set (from quests, rare treasure or lucky drops) and basic crafting, at least iron (or the tier after it).

## Side areas
- **The bog**, left of the route to the mountains, is a side-quest area. **Verona** (from Dawn's Journey) is there: a ranger who travels from land to land observing strange phenomena, drawn here by what is happening on Nuvatra. She is training alone in the swamp and asks the heroine to come back and tell her anything she finds. She can join the team later.
- **Pine Grove**, or the forest at the foot of the mountains: either Verona or a healer girl who simply lives on the island. *(Open.)*

## Cast notes
- **Heidi, the healer** (Pine Grove, the 5–10 quest-giver). Images in `~/Documents/art/Else/characters/Heidi`: brown braid, flower crown, dark teal dress with a white apron. A standard, well-written side character on the player's side. She goes into dangerous places and handles herself with defensive and healing abilities. As story flavour, not a combat mechanic, she can calm animals that have run amok, which is how she survives in the wilds right now.
- **Sylvie's brother:** his sword is the first weapon. He comes in later, perhaps as a stronger unit. A lesson from Dawn's Journey: an all-female cast makes world building and story harder, so the cast needs men in it.

## Decided 2026-11-09 (Agni): the Saints, the truth, the dragon
- **How evil the Saints are:** an order of faith with real ethics, by their own lights. They play fair wherever they can. They have no sovereignty on Nuvatra, so they won't use a heavy hand: Guinevere never uses more force than needed. Their ideology is warped, not their manners.
- **Claudia and Cygwin are not villains.** Claudia, *Whisper of the Tundra* (silver hair, blue and fur, aristocratic, tactical, warm); Cygwin, *War Cry of the Savannah* (tanned, blonde, red markings, a warrior). Both from Dawn's Journey. They live in a tent or makeshift house at the drill, as in Dawn's Journey.
- **Solis Sentinels on Nuvatra are not hostile:** they fight only if attacked.
- **The truth comes late,** from Veres (mountain cave), from Verona (talking it through), or from Marjane's emissary on the southern icy islands, who tells the player what the Saints are and that Guinevere is not who she seems.
- **Guinevere** meets the player once, at the shrine: a big moment. Then she leaves to make her journey south.
- **The scheme:** a dead dragon lies buried deep under Nuvatra. Its marrow is the island's life energy: it keeps the island thriving and keeps the seas and islands around it temperate. Guinevere wants to harness it. The icy islands forming in the south within 5–10 years is how Marjane noticed.
- **Marjane:** monarch of the southern seas, sovereign over half the world's abyssal plain. Incredibly strong, with no eyes on the ground, so she works through emissaries.
- **Time moves slowly for these people:** most are near demigods. Even Sylvie is an Aragorn: an ordinary-seeming ranger who turns out to be ~170.
- **Veres** (not Varys: voice typing) is *The Watching Wing*, a dark-winged angel who has watched the island longer than the Saints have built on it, and chose the mountain.
- **The bog** was never a story idea: it came from lily-pond tiles that read as a bog. Open to becoming lily ponds.

## The Theos swords, the elements, the goddesses (Agni, 2026-11-09)
- **What a Theos sword is:** an element or a concept lends part of its power to a sword, and through it to the wielder it chooses. The sword is sentient and chooses willingly. Most major characters have a sword as strong as a Theos sword, or come to own one.
- **Nature is split in three:** **Flora** (plants), **Fauna** (living creatures), **Terra** (rock, stone, the earth's formation). All three together would give full command of earth: a theoretical maximum nobody aims for.
- **Fire:** a primal **Fire** sword, and a weaker side piece, **Lightning**, which is in turn half of the sword of **Sky / Air**.
- **Ice** has its own sword.
- **Concepts** can have swords too: **Control** (Guinevere's).
- **The goddesses:** the high concepts (the elements, the sky, the moon, the sun) show up as goddesses. **Hela is near the top of that order, in the top three, and doesn't know it.** (The DJ bible already calls Hela *the Dawn*.)
- **Who holds what:**
  - **Verona:** Xochi, the Theos sword of **Flora**. Mother Nature gave it to her willingly, through what Verona did; she found it on the road, liked it, kept it ("it feels like an extension of my arm"). She learns what it is 2–3 regions later. In DJ, Xochi is an Earth sword with poison immunity.
  - **Guinevere:** the Theos sword of **Control**: ethos, logos and pathos used timely, routinely and with discipline, without meaning harm. Named *Her Majesty's Watch*.
  - **Claudia:** will own the Theos sword of **Ice**: a young noble mage, studious, diligent, bright; ice gives her the cold, unrelenting clarity of her vision.
  - **Cygwin:** will wield the Theos sword of **Fauna**: an empath who fights for anyone who can't; living creatures lend themselves to her.
- **Verona as a person:** a traveller who goes from land to land for new challenges. Disciplined, quick-tempered and irritable (especially in troubled places), adapts and learns from her own rash calls, means well, and warms up fast once she knows you.
- **DJ's elements:** Fire, Water, Wind, Earth (Nature), Light, Dark, with a 2× / 0.5× effectiveness chart (`dawns-journey/overview/elemental_effectiveness_matrix.csv`). Each character has one or two (the bible).

## Settled 2026-11-09 (Agni's answers)
- **Guinevere's two swords:** the **Saint's Greatsword** is her own, the one she's most attached to. **Her Majesty's Watch** (Control) is handed down to her by **Gabriella** later: the first time the player thwarts her plans, or the first time she makes a strong move.
- **The Fang** (the Saints' first, failed extraction: the visible fang of a huge buried dragon) comes in with the one redraw of the Nuvatra map, done at the very end once everything else is locked.
- **The Saints' fountain**, built to give back to the land, goes into Saltmere's map (no map redraw needed). It matters: it makes the Saints harder to dismiss.
- **Theos swords, in one line:** a force or ideal that people once worshipped as a god (ice, storms, famine, drought, control, persuasion, honesty, justice), given a will of its own, in the form of a sword; it chooses whom to back, and in that person's hands the force becomes a way of life. Not elements, but command of them. Ice and Lightning exist only as Theos swords, not as elements: Claudia is Water / Wind, and her sword is Ice.
- **Shadowhand** comes later, with a new character designed from scratch. *Akari* is dropped (too generic, and too close to the name Akiko).

## Theos swords and the powers, continued (Agni, 2026-11-09)
- **What people fear, desire or worship** can become a Theos sword: storms, famine, drought as well as ice or control. Not all of them turn up; they're a convenience for story, used where they fit.
- **Wizard and wand:** a strong person is strong without one; the right pairing (Elder-Wand level) is stupendous. A sword's strength follows **how big its concept is**: elemental swords are strong; feared ones are always strong but have counters and cracks in how they work. They are temperamental, can wander off, and don't work in some places or situations. (Example for much later: a desert bandit in the Indo-Persian lands with the Theos sword of **Drought**, who can open routes anywhere and keeps a safe haven for himself.)
- **Goddesses** are very strong even without their swords, and rarely fight: nobody wants to fight the goddess of nature, and she sees no need to fight anyone. To challenge a goddess, others must strengthen themselves first.
- **A sword can leave its wielder:** rarely, as a big story beat. It wants the best for its wielder, even if that means leaving and making them come and get it someday.
- **One Theos sword for each main character,** the player included. So far: Claudia (Ice), Cygwin (Fauna), Guinevere (Control, later), Verona (Flora, Xochi), the player, Hela, Veres. **Only Verona is in true control of hers**, which puts her at a very high level she hasn't realised. **Veres has one and never uses it.**
- **Legendary swords in play:** "cracked" abilities: one-shots, map-wide attacks, with long cooldowns and steep requirements.
- **Hela:** has her own sword, which gives her control of Hell, and believes that's all she's meant to be; she doesn't know there's a Theos sword meant for her. Long ago a **concordat** gave Hela Hell, while "God" (whatever presents itself as God later: the head of the Saints, or something higher) took the heavens, the skies and the earth. Now there's an **overflow of souls into Hell**; Hela is furious and on earth to find out why. She is strengthening herself, a transformation from a girl with light purple hair to the reinstated Queen of Hell, ready to challenge the heavens (stages in `extras/sprites_for_later/`).
- **Veres:** archangel level, a millennia-old friend of Hela, about equal to Hela's power now. She didn't like an archangel's disciplined life: a wild card, a dilettante, she left the angel training. Kind, smart, very clever, knows the land.
- **To reconcile:** the secret above says Hela purged her good part to go to war with the world; here she's on earth investigating the soul overflow. Both can be true (the investigation is why she came; the war is where it leads), but it needs one line deciding it.
