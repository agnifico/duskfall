# Area brief: Saltmere and the Drill (levels 15–22)

From Agni's answers, 2026-11-09. Phase 1 built the same day; phase 2 (the shoals, the Lily Ponds) to come.

- **Area:** the north-east shore. Two maps: **Saltmere**, a fishing village (no fighting: the first people-only point, the hub), and **the Drill**, the Saints' headland beside it. Later: **the offshore shoals** by Saltmere's boat (20–25), and **the Lily Ponds** (side area, Verona).
- **Look:** the land map's art as it stands: village houses west, the drill in the middle, docks south-east, mountains south-west.
- **Levels:** Saltmere none (no enemies); the Drill 17–22.
- **Opens when:** Sylvie's *The West Fields* at level 15 (tag `drill-open`).
- **Story:** the village was there first. The Saints leased the headland from it, through Sister Aurelie, the priestess they took in as an orphan and helped lead the village to prosperity. They pay fairly. **The village is split:** Brann likes the coin; Marisol blames the drill for the fish leaving. Claudia and Cygwin (decent people who believe the survey story) want the village's goodwill back, so their daily tasks send the player to run errands for the villagers. Cygwin gave herself to the Saints as the finest thing her tribe had to offer (Queen Amanirenas). The Saints work like a church: they gather resources, then hand them out and take people in.
- **Enemies:** Solis sentinels (level 20, still: they fight only if attacked); crabscallions (16) on the shore and boaring captains (18) in the scrub, unruly mercenaries working around the drill; forest prowlers from the treeline.
- **People:** Sister Aurelie (priestess), Claudia, Cygwin, Marisol (fisherwoman), Brann (smith and trader: the first shop), Old Tova (net-mender).
- **Story beats:** *Saltmere* (meet Aurelie) → *Guests on the Headland* (meet Claudia and Cygwin; discipline the boar-men; iron ore for the bracing; opens the villagers' daily quests) → *Where the Fish Went* (level 19: the bay's water is cold at the bottom in summer; Tova's "warm heart" story; points to Verona). The truth about the Saints comes much later (Veres, Verona, Marjane's emissary).
- **Leaves with:** Iron, smithed from iron veins (Mining 5) at the Crafting screen; the first shop (Brann) for food, potions and the three Traveller's weapons, and buying materials; coins finally have a use.
- **Unlocks next:** the Lily Ponds (`ponds-hint`): a burnt stretch of forest that lightning took, now healing, with water filling the craters. Verona is part person, part boss there: cold, quiet, competitive, and she will knock you out until you put the work in. The shoals: hints of something massive under the water; Marjane's sirens patrol the island like dementors, and the shark soldiers and aquamech sharks are hers too; the inkjet ray and coral eyes are wild. Sylvie's farmstead is where farming opens one day.

**Phase 2 (built 2026-11-09):** Lily Ponds and the Shoals as above. Verona's portrait is cut from the leaf-crowned ranger picture (`extras/sprites_for_later/a2xUf.jpg`), a guess to confirm.
