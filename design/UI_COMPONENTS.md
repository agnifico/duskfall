# UI components

A checklist for drawing the UI. ★ = needed for Chapter 1. The rest comes later.

## Everything, every screen
- ★ **Button states:** default, hover, pressed, disabled, locked, selected, "new" badge
- ★ **Cost chip** on actions: free · 1⚡ · 3⚡ · not enough
- ★ **Panel frame** that stretches to any size (9-slice), a divider, a scrollbar
- ★ **Text styles:** title, heading, body, small, numbers
- ★ **Colour roles:** primary, secondary, energy, free (green), danger, locked (grey), rarity tiers

## Always on screen (HUD)
- ★ Portrait button (opens Character)
- ★ Energy: bar or pips, plus a refill timer
- ★ Day and time of day
- ★ Location title or breadcrumb: Nuvatra › Arrival Beach
- ★ Menu buttons: Bag, Quests, Character, Map, Settings, each with a notification dot
- ★ Quest tracker: the current objective, small
- Currency display

## Map (world and region)
- ★ Map frame, zoom in and out, recentre
- ★ Pins: zone label, point (gathering · secrets and enemies · people), locked, quest marker (! and ?), you are here
- ★ Pin card on tap: name, kind, what's there
- ★ Region name label
- Unexplored overlay

## Hex point
- ★ Hex terrain set, with hover, path and target highlights
- ★ Player token
- ★ Node states: available · used up (regrows) · locked (needs a tool tier) · tier badge
- ★ Markers: enemy, person, hidden find, way out
- ★ Route preview: a path line with a cost chip
- ★ Fog for unexplored hexes
- ★ Action panel: what you can do on this hex, each with its cost chip
- ★ Floating gains (+2 Wood) and the herb-find popup

## Dialogue
- ★ Dialogue box: name plate, portrait with expressions, text, a "continue" marker
- ★ Choice buttons (2–4)
- ★ Speech bubble over a token or a person
- Emote icons: ! ? heart …

## Quests
- ★ Quest log: tabs (active, done), quest card (title, who gave it, current objective, progress 4/10), locked "???" card
- ★ Toasts: quest received, objective done, quest complete, plus a reward panel

## Bag
- ★ Grid of slots: icon, stack count, rarity border, new-item dot
- ★ Category tabs: all, materials, tools, food, quest
- ★ Item detail: name, type, rarity, description, what it's used for
- Capacity counter
- ★ Item icons (materials, woods, ores, herbs)

## Character and skills
- ★ Character card: portrait, name, World Resonance
- ★ Skills: Mining, Woodcutting, Herblore, each with level, XP bar and next unlock
- ★ Tool slots (axe, pick) showing the tier
- ★ Level-up toast

## Crafting
- ★ Recipe list; recipe card with inputs (have/need), output and a Craft button

## System
- ★ Story panel viewer for the storm opening: full-screen picture, caption, next
- ★ Confirm dialog
- ★ Title screen; settings (sound, text speed)
- Loading screen
